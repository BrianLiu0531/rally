Rally

A real-time two-player game over WebSockets. The server runs the only simulation; your browser predicts your own paddle so it feels instant, and you can drop your connection mid-match and carry on.

Controls: W/S or ↑/↓, or drag on touch screens. First to 5.

    How ranking works

    Quick matches between two players are rated with Elo. Results come from the server’s own simulation, so there is nothing for a client to submit or fake. To stop rating farming, a match is unrated when both players are on the same network or the same browser, when it is abandoned in the first 10 seconds, when the loser never moved, or after 3 rated games against the same opponent in a day. Bot and private-room matches are always unrated.

    This server has no database, so ratings reset when it restarts.

    Netcode

    Add latency, then turn prediction off: your paddle starts lagging behind your keys. Drop the connection and the match pauses until you are back.
